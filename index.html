The idea

Five radial-engine airframes, one coastal operation.

Nothing in the hangar that Port Hardy's strips, floats, and gravel bars can't handle. The Beaver goes where there's no strip at all, the Beech 18 scouts the tight fields, the DC-3 carries the scheduled roster, the C-46 takes the loads the Beech can't, and the DC-6 flies the long coastal hauls where payload beats speed.
