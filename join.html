How the operation runs

Fly With Us

Rivian Air Lines flies inside OnAir Company on the Stratus world. Here's how a job moves from the board to a logbook entry.

Step by step

From the job board to the ramp

01 — Source
Jobs are pulled from the shared OnAir logistics market out of Port Hardy — cargo and passenger work alike.
02 — Match
Each job is matched to the tail best suited to it: the Beech 18 for tight strips, the DC-3 for scheduled pax, the C-46 and DC-6 for heavier freight.
03 — Fly
Legs are flown live in the simulator, loaded and manifested the way the aircraft would have been in service.
04 — Log
Completed flights post to the company record on GA Realistic, building the operation's history one leg at a time.
