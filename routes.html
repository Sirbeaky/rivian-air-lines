Where we fly

The near-term network stays close to home — short coastal hops well suited to the Beech 18 and DC-3, with the DC-6 and C-46 picking up the longer or heavier runs as job volume builds.

Short coastal feeder

Port Hardy to Port McNeill and Bella Bella — small-party charter and light freight for the Beech 18.

Regional scheduled

Campbell River and Comox — the DC-3's scheduled pax and mixed-load runs down the island.

Mainline / heavy freight

Longer coastal legs toward Vancouver — DC-6 and C-46 territory once volume justifies the larger tails.

Actual jobs are matched day to day through OnAir's shared job market, so the exact city pairs flown shift with demand — this is the shape of the network, not a fixed timetable.
